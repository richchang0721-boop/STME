# STME — Structured Multi-State Transition & Evaluation

STME is not a decision tool.

It is a system for preserving decision structure under uncertainty.

Most AI systems generate answers.
STME prevents premature collapse of decision space.
---

## Live Demo

Try the interactive demo here:

👉 https://www.pida-lab.com/demo

This demo does not provide answers.
It exposes internal states, structural pressures, and possible transitions behind a decision.

---
## What STME Does

- Preserves multiple concurrent decision states
- Identifies structural pressures and constraints
- Ranks transitions without forcing convergence
- Makes decision processes observable

---

## Positioning

STME is not designed to replace human decision-making.

It is designed to:

- reduce premature decisions
- expose hidden constraints
- maintain structural awareness under uncertainty

---

## Relationship to PIDA and CIP

STME serves as an operational layer built on top of:

- Cognitive Integrity Protocol (CIP)
- PIDA (Pre-Incident Responsibility Architecture)

CIP defines boundaries.
PIDA defines interaction structure.
STME makes decision structure observable.
